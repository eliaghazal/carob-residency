# Staff access to customer data

Owner: Compliance.

- Supervisors and admins may open any customer's profile, wallets and transactions.
- Agents may open a customer's profile, wallets and transactions only while they hold an open
  conversation with that customer assigned to them.
- Every view of a customer by staff is written to the audit log.
- KYC decisions and transfer reversals are for supervisors and admins only.
